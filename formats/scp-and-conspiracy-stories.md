# SCP and conspiracy stories

Locked by the user on 19 November 2026 as the second MotionLab format.

Tell an incident or investigation through a person with a clear objective. Build the progression around an unsettling event, discovery of a rule or connection, an attempt to respond, and a consequence that changes the viewer's understanding. Give the viewer enough information to anticipate danger or form a theory before the reveal.

Use the treatment the story needs. Options include an encounter staged from inside the scene, a compact explanation of one unsettling concept, and a reconstruction assembled from evidence. Follow researchers, guards, witnesses or investigators when their choices carry the story.

For an evidence investigation, photographs, Polaroids, floor plans, recordings, maps and documents can be handled on a detective's desk or board. Each piece must change what can be inferred. Enter a reconstructed event through a relevant detail, then return to the evidence when its meaning changes. Time the reveal so the film advances once the viewer understands it.

Choose atmosphere, suspense, tragedy, discovery or dark humour to suit the subject. Make ordinary routines and geography clear enough for a violation to register. Sound, silence, framing and withheld information carry suspense. Keep one recognizable subject or clue through changes in visual treatment.

For SCP adaptations, consult the original article and relevant incident or tale. Distinguish adaptation staging and invented material from source-established events. Preserve the relevant author attribution, source links and licence terms. Check image rights separately.

For real-world conspiracy subjects, distinguish verified events, attributed allegations, disputed interpretations and fiction. Verify the provenance and context of photographs, quotations and documents. Label reconstructions and generated evidence illustrations. A compelling reveal must be supported by the evidence. An invented conspiracy can be presented as an explicitly fictional story.

Use MotionLab's established production workflow and choose relevant visual treatments by their story purpose. The user handles narration and captions under the current standing preference. Script research and drafting remain at the text stage until production is requested.

The user may invoke this format as "SCP/conspiracy story" or "evidence investigation".
