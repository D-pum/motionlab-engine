Explain how a machine, mechanism or physical process works through observable cause and effect. The film must be understandable through its visuals and sound. Use a clear opening action or question, reveal the relevant parts, follow the transfer of motion, energy or material, and return to the complete working system.

Use a stable spatial model, recognizable components and consistent directions and colours. Show an operating cycle and its important phases. Use cutaways, transparent housings, exploded views, cross-sections, slow motion, sectional diagrams and motivated camera moves where they clarify an otherwise hidden action. Establish the whole before isolating a part and show how the part reconnects.

Time sound to meaningful physical actions. Minimal functional labels, directional arrows and phase indicators may identify parts and state changes. Choose readable pacing and give the viewer time to connect an input to its result. The user handles any narration and captions under the standing preference.

Verify the mechanism against appropriate sources. State simplifications and conceptual representations in the production notes. Preserve the operation and relationships the explanation depends on. Use the established MotionLab production workflow and select visual treatments for clarity.
